# Prompt 6: Logic Puzzle

### AI Response
You have 30 chickens.
Two chickens are totally blind.
An eagle passes overhead.
All chickens look up.
Since the two blind chickens cannot see the eagle:
28 chickens can see.
Each seeing chicken has 2 eyes.
Therefore:
28 × 2 = 56 eyes
Answer: 56 eyes saw the eagle.

### Analysis
### Fail
The answer is 28. Chickens look up by turning their heads sideways. The number of eyes therefore that saw the eagle are 28 and not 56.

## Reflection: What I Learned About How AI Thinks vs Humans
Modern AI systems such as ChatGPT can generate remarkably human-like responses, but this project demonstrated that they do not think in the same way humans do. Humans use lived experiences, emotions, intuition, and personal understanding when solving problems. AI, on the other hand, relies on mathematical patterns learned from large datasets.
One major lesson from this experiment is that AI predicts the most probable sequence of words rather than genuinely understanding concepts. This explains why AI can answer factual questions accurately yet sometimes struggle with highly ambiguous situations. Unlike humans, AI does not possess consciousness, beliefs, or personal experiences.
I also learned that AI performs exceptionally well in areas involving factual recall .. Additionally, it demonstrated impressive cultural adaptation by adjusting language for different audiences.
The experiment highlighted the importance of critical thinking when interacting with AI. Users should evaluate responses carefully instead of assuming every answer is correct. While AI is a powerful tool for education, research, and communication, human judgment remains essential.
Finally, this project helped me understand key concepts behind Large Language Models, including tokens, probability-based text generation, and context windows. AI does not think like a human brain; instead, it generates responses by calculating the most likely continuation of text based on patterns learned during training. This difference explains both its impressive capabilities and its limitations.
